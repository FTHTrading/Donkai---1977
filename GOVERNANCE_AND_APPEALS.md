# DONK AI Governance & Appeals Framework

**Framework Code:** `GOV-APP-1.0`  
**Purpose:** Role-based human governance, sybil resistance, and dispute resolution.

---

## 1. Functional Governance Roles

- **Author:** Submits primary human remembrance.
- **Corroborator:** Submits independent blind witness account.
- **Evidence Custodian:** Maintains physical or high-resolution digital artifacts.
- **Peer Reviewer:** Evaluates submissions against open rubrics.
- **Translator:** Attests to cross-language derivative accuracy.
- **Appeals Panel:** Resolves disputed historical-support classifications.
- **Protocol Steward:** Administers technical upgrades, parameter settings, and non-factual treasury grants.

---

## 2. Non-Transferable Human Pass (SBT)

Participation roles are bound to `DonkaiHumanPass.sol`. Credentials cannot be bought, sold, traded, or transferred. Reputation and review rights cannot be purchased with capital.

---

## 3. Appeals Protocol

1. **Window:** 90 days following initial `ReviewAssessment` commitment.
2. **Standing:** Open to the original author or any verified contributor with new evidence.
3. **Quorum:** Minimum 3 independent Appeals Panel members with conflict-of-interest disclosures.
