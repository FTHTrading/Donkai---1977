# DONK AI Privacy & Consent Framework

**Policy Code:** `PRIV-CNS-1.0`  
**Core Rule:** Privacy is an architectural requirement, not an optional preference.

---

## 1. Six Visibility Modes

1. **`public`:** Full narrative and metadata indexed on the Memory Drift Atlas.
2. **`reviewer-only`:** Encrypted payload accessible only to credentialed curators until verification milestone is reached.
3. **`trusted-circle`:** Compartmentalized decryption keys for family or verified local community members.
4. **`delayed-public`:** Time-locked encryption released automatically after an embargo window (e.g., 90 days).
5. **`aggregate-only`:** Narrative remains private; metadata contributes only to statistical clustering.
6. **`private`:** Encrypted client-side with AES-GCM-256 before leaving the user's browser.

---

## 2. Minor Protection & Sensitive Content

- Memories regarding individuals who were minors at the time of the event require explicit identity redactions.
- Trauma, medical information, and non-public allegations require mandatory reviewer-only staging or aggregate-only modes.
