# DONK AI Charter: Foundational Principles of Human Remembrance

**Status:** Adopted Protocol Constitution  
**Jurisdiction:** Global Decentralized Human Memory Network  

---

## Preamble

We, the contributors, archivists, researchers, and witnesses of the DONK AI network, establish this Charter to preserve human remembrance in an era of synthetic uncertainty, digital ephemerality, and algorithmic distortion.

Remembrance is an essential human right. The preservation of what individuals and communities recall—regardless of whether it conforms to official archives or popular consensus—is vital to historical understanding, cultural identity, and human dignity.

---

## Article I: The Inviolability of Human Authorship

1. **Human Primacy:** Only human beings may author primary remembrance statements.
2. **Prohibition of AI Testimony:** Artificial intelligence systems are strictly prohibited from generating, synthesizing, replacing, or simulating first-person human recollection.
3. **Preservation of Original Prose:** The canonical record shall preserve the author's exact prose, dialect, slang, spelling, and phrasing without automated correction.

---

## Article II: Epistemic Distinctions & Truth Integrity

1. **The Epistemic Separation:** The protocol shall visibly maintain the boundary between subjective recall, independent witness corroboration, artifact integrity, and historical support.
2. **Rejection of Capital Truth:** No financial stake, prediction bet, governance token, or economic consideration shall determine the historical veracity or classification of a record.
3. **Rejection of Popularity:** Upvotes, likes, reposts, or viral sentiment shall never alter or validate a historical record.
4. **Mandatory Uncertainty Visibility:** Every public record shall transparently declare what remains unresolved, conflicting, or unevidenced.

---

## Article III: The Right to Privacy & Consent

1. **Consent Primacy:** Contributors retain sovereign authority over the visibility, pseudonymity, and retention policy of their recollections.
2. **Client-Side Sovereignty:** Restricted and private records must be encrypted on the user's device prior to transmission.
3. **Right of Retraction:** Authors maintain the irrevocable right to amend, clarify, or retract their submissions through cryptographically linked tombstone records.
4. **Minor & Sensitive Protection:** High-risk testimonies involving minors, trauma, active conflict, or private medical details require strict access compartmentalization.

---

## Article IV: Blind Corroboration Integrity

1. **Anti-Anchoring Guarantee:** The protocol shall provide mechanisms for independent witnesses to record sealed recollections against neutral discovery parameters prior to viewing primary testimonies.
2. **Neutral Discovery:** Discovery prompts must never leak leading narrative details or specific witness claims.

---

## Article V: Open & Disclosed Methodology

1. **Versioned Rubrics:** All institutional and peer reviews must reference publicly verifiable, versioned evaluation rubrics.
2. **Conflict Disclosure:** Reviewers must cryptographically commit to conflict-of-interest disclosures alongside their assessments.
3. **Open Appeals:** Any classification may be appealed during an active review window upon presentation of new evidence or methodological dispute.
