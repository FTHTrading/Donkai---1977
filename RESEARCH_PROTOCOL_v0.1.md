# DONK AI Research Protocol (v0.1)

**Purpose:** Standard operating methodology for academic researchers, oral historians, and cultural archivists exploring collective recall, memory drift, and the Mandela Effect within DONK AI.

---

## 1. Ethical Guidelines

1. **Non-Pathologizing Approach:** Memory drift is studied as a natural cognitive, linguistic, and cultural phenomenon, not as psychological failure or proof of alternate realities.
2. **Subject Protection:** Researchers must respect the `ConsentManifest` of every record. Records flagged as `reviewer-only` or `aggregate-only` must never have raw prose or identity details published outside authorized bounds.
3. **Harm Mitigation:** Trauma-related memories and active geopolitical conflict testimonies require IRB-compliant handling protocols.

---

## 2. Research Data Pipelines

Researchers may query:
- **Blind Overlap Metrics:** Cluster densities of independent witness accounts against neutral discovery tags.
- **Geographic Dispersion Maps:** Regional concentration vs global diffusion of recall variations.
- **Temporal Lag Curves:** Correlation between claimed event date and commitment timestamp.

---

## 3. Mandatory Disclaimers

Any published research utilizing DONK AI data MUST state:
> *"Data obtained via DONK AI establishes cryptographic submission provenance and independent recall clustering. It does not constitute verified historical reality or institutional consensus on event veracity."*
