# DONK AI Assistance & Usage Disclosure Policy

**Policy Code:** `AI-DSC-1.0`  
**Purpose:** Defining strict operational boundaries for Artificial Intelligence within the Human Remembrance Protocol.

---

## 1. Absolute Prohibitions

Artificial Intelligence systems are strictly forbidden from:
- **Authoring Primary Testimony:** No AI-generated or synthetic accounts may be committed as human remembrance.
- **Silent Rewriting:** No automated grammar, vocabulary, dialect, or tone alterations.
- **Voting or Consensus Staking:** AI agents have zero voting weight in historical support assessments.
- **Truth Determination:** No AI output may be designated as a truth verdict.

---

## 2. Permitted AI Support Roles

AI tools may be used exclusively for:
1. **Accessibility Aids:** Speech-to-text dictation tools operated directly by human witnesses.
2. **Translation Assistance:** Draft translations, provided they are explicitly reviewed, signed, and attested by bilingual human translators.
3. **OCR & Indexing:** Text extraction from scanned physical documents.
4. **Discovery Tagging:** Proposing neutral topical tags for blind corroboration queues.
