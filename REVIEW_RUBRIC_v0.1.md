# DONK AI Review Rubric (v0.1)

**Standard:** `RUBRIC-REV-0.1`  
**Purpose:** Standardized evaluation framework for credentialed curators and peer reviewers.

---

## 1. Evaluation Criteria

1. **Submission Provenance (CRIT-1):** Valid LPS-1 Merkle root, verified signer attestation, and unaltered canonical JSON.
2. **Artifact Support (CRIT-2):** Presence of Tier 1 or Tier 2 contemporaneous physical or documentary evidence.
3. **Blind Corroboration (CRIT-3):** Minimum threshold of 3 independent, sealed blind witness accounts with consistent factual overlap.
4. **Conflict & Anomaly Analysis (CRIT-4):** Evaluation of contradicting archival records, timeline impossibilities, or retrofitted narratives.

---

## 2. Classification Definitions

- **Historically Supported:** Validated contemporaneous artifact evidence aligns with corroborated witness testimonies.
- **Partially Supported:** Corroborated witness accounts with subsequent or circumstantial documentation; minor discrepancies remain.
- **Conflicting Evidence:** Documented contemporaneous records directly contradict the remembered details.
- **Unresolved:** Plausible witness accounts without surviving physical or documentary records; open for ongoing submissions.
- **Insufficient Evidence:** Uncorroborated single-witness claim without supporting artifacts.
- **Retracted by Author:** Withdrawn by original submitter.
