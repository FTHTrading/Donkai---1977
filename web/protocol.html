DONK AI CANONICAL SPECIFICATION

Protocol Charter & Standards

The official architecture, epistemic constraints, and cryptographic commitment definitions of DONK AI: The Human Remembrance Protocol.

1. Non-Negotiable Epistemic Principles

DONK AI makes uncertainty visible. The product never claims that a blockchain, a token, AI, voting, or prediction stakes prove a memory is objectively true.

Core Distinction Formula:

Popularity ≠ Corroboration ≠ Evidence Integrity ≠ Historical Support
Stake ≠ Truth

What a valid DONK AI record establishes:
  • A signer submitted a specified canonical record.
  • The record existed no later than its chain-anchored timestamp.
  • The committed record has not changed when its Merkle root is recomputed.
  • An artifact was supplied, hashed, attributed, and linked to a record.
  • Multiple witnesses independently submitted accounts under blind-first conditions.
What a record CANNOT establish:
  • That a subjective memory is objectively true.
  • That an artifact's interpretation is complete or singular.
  • That token ownership, stake, or public consensus equates to historical reality.

2. LPS-1 Living Provenance Standard (v2.0)

LPS-1 replaces static document hashing with typed, domain-separated cryptographic commitments for human remembrance records, context manifests, consent manifests, and evidence bundles. 

leaf_hash   = SHA256( "DONKAI:LPS1:LEAF::v1:" || canonical_bytes )
parent_hash = SHA256( "DONKAI:LPS1:NODE:v1:" || left_hash || right_hash )
root_hash   = SHA256( "DONKAI:LPS1:ROOT::v1:" || tree_root )

Canonicalization Rules

  • UTF-8 only • Unicode NFC normalized
  • Deterministic JSON serialization with lexicographically sorted object keys
  • No insignificant whitespace • Strict RFC 3339 UTC timestamps
  • Original human prose preserved without auto-correction, grammar filtering, or dialect alteration

3. AI Assistance & Disclosure Policy

DONK AI is a human remembrance network. AI tools may assist with optional discovery indexing, accessibility aids, OCR transcription, and translation review, but AI must never:

  • Author a primary remembrance statement
  • Replace, silently rewrite, or fabricate human testimony
  • Vote or stake on memory records
  • Determine historical truth verdicts

4. Privacy, Consent & Retention

Privacy is an architectural requirement. The protocol supports six granular visibility modes:

  • Public: Indexed on the Memory Drift Atlas.
  • Reviewer-Only: Restricted to credentialed curators until verified.
  • Trusted-Circle: Access-controlled for families and local communities.
  • Delayed-Public: Sealed for an agreed embargo period.
  • Aggregate-Only: Included only in research statistics and theme clustering.
  • Private: Client-side encrypted with AES-GCM-256 before upload.
