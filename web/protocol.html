GOVERNANCE & METHODOLOGY

The DONK AI Charter & Epistemic Methodology

DONK AI is a privacy-aware, human-governed protocol designed to preserve first-person testimony, physical evidence provenance, and blind independent corroboration.

Core Epistemic Equation
Popularity ≠ Corroboration ≠ Evidence Integrity ≠ Historical Support
Stake ≠ Truth

1. Explicit Boundaries: What DONK AI Proves

DONK AI does not use financial stakes, popularity voting, or AI generation to settle historical truth. The platform provides transparent provenance mechanisms:

Status Indicator What It Proves What It Does NOT Mean
Content Hash Verified The digital payload matches the 32-byte hash committed at submission. Does not prove the depicted object is historically authentic or that the photo was taken at the claimed date.
CID Recorded The content-addressed payload resolves to the committed byte sequence when available. Does not guarantee eternal availability or that the content is universally accepted as truth.
Merkle Inclusion Verified The item was cryptographically included in the signed record bundle. Does not prove the testimony is complete, unconflicted, or legally dispositive.
Submission Signed A designated signing identity committed this specific canonical record no later than the block timestamp. Does not establish that the author's memory is accurate or free from cognitive bias.
Historical Support Assigned Reviewers evaluated submitted artifacts and witness accounts against Rubric v0.1. Does not mean the classification is beyond appeal, universally complete, or an absolute truth verdict.

2. Living Provenance Standard 1 (LPS-1 v2.0)

LPS-1 represents memories as typed, canonical Merkle trees. Rather than storing raw sensitive text on public ledgers, DONK AI calculates domain-separated SHA-256 leaf hashes for each distinct record component:

  • Statement Leaf: Original narrative text, language tag, and human authorship attestation.
  • Context Leaf: Broad geographical and chronological metadata used for blind discovery.
  • Consent Leaf: Visibility policies, access epochs, retention terms, and pseudonymity rules.
  • Evidence Bundle Leaf: Content hashes, custody metadata, and AI disclosure statements for physical/digital artifacts.
  • Version Graph Leaf: Lineage pointers to parent records for corrections, translations, or retractions.

3. Operational 2-Phase Blind Corroboration

To eliminate priming, social contagion, and bandwagon bias, independent witness accounts are collected via a cryptographic two-phase commit-reveal protocol:

  1. Discovery Phase: The contributor sees only broad parameters (e.g. "Austin, TX • 1978 • Arcade") without access to the target narrative.
  2. Local Sealing: The contributor authors their recollection locally. The browser computes a sealed root: SHA-256("DONKAI:LPS1:BLIND_CORROBORATION:v1:" + salt + narrative).
  3. On-Chain Commit: The sealed root is committed to DonkaiBlindCorroboration.sol.
  4. Reveal & Review: Only after the commitment is anchored on-chain does the reveal phase occur, enabling transparent peer review without prior contamination.
