THE DONK AI MANIFESTO

Human Memory Is Not a Token.

It is a signal: personal, cultural, fallible, emotional, and sometimes historically important.

DONK AI gives people a way to preserve that signal without forcing it into a false binary of "true" or "false." A remembrance can be signed without being publicly exposed. Evidence can be linked without being confused with testimony. Independent accounts can converge—or conflict—without a crowd, an algorithm, or money deciding what reality must have been.

Why We Use Web3

Because people deserve portable authorship, verifiable provenance, consent-aware access, and records that cannot be silently rewritten by centralized platforms.

What We Reject

We do not use Web3 to sell truth. We do not use popularity to overwrite minority memory. We do not use AI to pronounce judgment on a human life.

What We Preserve

DONK AI preserves what was remembered, what was submitted, what can be historically supported, and what remains unresolved.
