Non-Negotiable Epistemic Rules

DONK AI is not an artificial-memory system or a speculative truth market. We preserve these distinctions technically and visibly:

Popularity ≠ Truth

Likes, upvotes, or token stakes never alter a historical record. A widely shared recollection is documented as a cultural convergence, not an objective historical fact.

No AI Authoring

AI may assist with transcription, translation review, or discovery metadata, but AI is strictly barred from authoring, editing, voting, or judging human testimony.

Making Uncertainty Visible

Every record displays a structured 5-panel ledger: What is Remembered, What is Recalled, What Evidence Exists, What is Supported, and What Remains Unresolved.
