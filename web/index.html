How the Chain Actually Works

  
The satirical framing wraps three technically real pillars, all shipping in the v0.1.0 Rust workspace with 28/28 tests passing and a warning-free build.

  

    

      
1 LPS-1 Provenance

      
Every document, letter, or lore entry is paragraph-split, SHA-256 hashed into a binary Merkle tree, and audited across 58 discrete named checks. The root anchors to Polygon mainnet (Chain ID 137) and Bitcoin OpenTimestamps.

      
Not a marketing number: the 58 checks are enumerated as real programmatic assertions in donkai-lps1::run_all_58_checks — count them yourself.

      

        
O(log n)inclusion proofs

        
58 / 58audit checks pass

      

    

    

      
2 Proof-of-Stubbornness

      
Validator vote weight = stake × blocks_unmoved × tier. Selling within 30 days makes you a PaperHand (0×). Holding through a 30% drawdown makes you Stubborn (1×). Holding through a 90% drawdown makes you UltraAsinine (10×).

      
Consensus finalizes under strict 2/3 BFT — Asinine Fault Tolerance is the marketing name; the math is agreeing × 3 ≥ total × 2 with saturating u128 arithmetic.

      

        
10×max tier multiplier

        
≥ 2/3BFT supermajority

      

    

    

      
3 PolicyGuard + PQC

      
AI agent actions are gated by D0–D5 risk tiers. D4/D5 require a 2-of-N quorum of ML-DSA-87 (FIPS 204) signatures against a whitelisted validator set — the signatures are cryptographically verified, not counted.

      
Validator peer comms are wrapped in ML-KEM-1024 (FIPS 203) key encapsulation to defeat "harvest now, decrypt later" adversaries.

      

        
FIPS 204ML-DSA-87 sigs

        
FIPS 203ML-KEM-1024 KEM

      

    

  






  
Mint a Relic

  
Any wallet can mint a historical Relic NFT — a photo of your original Pet Rock, a receipt for horse armor, a physical roll of Skee-Ball tickets from 1994. The preview below runs the LPS-1 Merkle root and raw CIDv1 derivations in your browser, using the same algorithms as the Rust crates. The output is byte-for-byte identical to what donkai-lps1 and donkai-ipfs produce on the chain.

  

    
    
    
    
    
    

      
      
    

    
On-chain minting will be wired to DonkaiTimelineRegistry.mintRelic() in the next release. For now, take the derived root and CID to any wallet that speaks Donkai devnet 1977. Duplicate roots are rejected on-chain — same document, same relic.

  






  
Format War Arena

  
Three canonical matchups seeded on the arena. Each maps to a real matchup in DonkaiFormatWar.sol. Stakes below are mocked in your browser — click either side to see the pull-payment formula redistribute the split.

  

  

    The winner claims their principal plus a pro-rata share of the losing pool:
    payout = principal + (principal × losing_pool ÷ winning_pool).
    No unbounded loops, pull-payment only, ReentrancyGuard on every value flow.

The Stack

  
Everything below ships in the v0.1.0 tag. 28 / 28 Rust tests pass. Three Solidity contracts compile clean under EIP-170. No unwired dependencies, no tautological verification fields, no placeholder CIDs.

ML-DSA-87 signatures (FIPS 204) + ML-KEM-1024 key exchange (FIPS 203) via pqcrypto-*.
